The Greek Letters are used widely in LaTeX mathematical equation typesetting. Due to a long time of cultural spreading and mutation, some letters has been developed different script forms. Overleaf provides a excellent guide \cite{overleaf:mathsymbols} on Greek Letters that helped me a lot. However, it's incomplete after I discussing with Claude AI. So, it's necessary to record a complete list for understanding and daily practical writing in LaTeX. The basic information is from the Wiki Page \cite{wiki:greek_alphabet}.
