Complete List of Greek Letters in Latex

The Greek Letters are used widely in LaTeX mathematical equation typesetting. Due to a long history of cultural transmission and regional variation, some letters have developed distinct script forms. Overleaf provides an excellent guide [1] on Greek Letters that helped me considerably. However, after discussing with Claude AI, I found it incomplete. This article records a complete list for understanding and daily practical writing in LaTeX. The basic information is from the Wikipedia article on the Greek alphabet [2].
